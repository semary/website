03 / Approach

How the work actually runs

Start with the problem, make the trade-offs explicit, and keep learning after release. I adapt the depth and pace to the team and the decisions in front of us.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, market and competitor analysis, constraint mapping. Finding out what is actually being asked for, which is rarely what was first said.

02

Define

Business requirements document, use cases, user stories with acceptance criteria, user flows, and a clickable prototype. A shared reference for business, design, and engineering.

03

Deliver

Sprint cadence, backlog ownership, QA planning, release sequencing, and the stakeholder communication that keeps scope from quietly expanding.

04

Measure

Instrumentation, funnel and cohort review, experiment backlog. Deciding what the next release should contain based on evidence rather than the loudest voice.

06 / A little context

About

I have spent fourteen years in technology, the last four in fintech and digital banking. I work with the details that connect a customer journey to a functioning system: payment rules, wallet and card lifecycles, lending flows, and the requirements teams need to build them.

Before fintech I worked in edtech, content, and community, and I founded an Arabic blockchain publication that reached about ten thousand readers a month. Those experiences still shape how I work. A big part of product management is getting people who disagree to agree on what is being built, and I learned that running a volunteer community across ten cities long before I learned it running a sprint.

I’m based in Cairo. I work in Arabic and English and have delivered for clients across the GCC, Europe, Turkey, and the United States. It has taught me to ask what changes from one market to the next, rather than assume the same product decisions will work everywhere.
