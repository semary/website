About

Clarity is the product.

Fintech & payments Digital lending AI products Discovery & BRDs Roadmapping Go-to-market

I'm Mohamed Sameer, a product manager based in Cairo. I build fintech, payments, and AI products, translating complex banking and regulatory requirements into clear, usable experiences that ship and create measurable value.

Over 14 years in technology, 4 of them in banking and fintech (payments, wallets, cards, and digital lending), I've led cross-functional teams through the full product lifecycle, from discovery to delivery, including mobile apps, customer portals, and API integrations. My work sits at the intersection of business and engineering: I'm as comfortable writing a BRD and shaping a roadmap as I am sitting with developers to make sure what gets built actually works.

Most products don't fail because the idea is bad; they fail because the requirements are unclear, priorities shift, and teams lose alignment.

My job is to remove that ambiguity and get from idea to scope to shipped product with focus and speed.
